About ORBIS

ORBIS is an advanced satellite analytics platform dedicated to tracking and analyzing urban sprawl through unsupervised machine learning. By utilizing K-Means clustering, the system segments high-resolution satellite imagery into distinct land classes, allowing for the precise measurement of urban growth, vegetation health, and infrastructure expansion over time.

Our mission is to provide researchers, urban planners, and environmentalists with an intuitive, powerful tool to visualize temporal changes in land use, empowering data-driven decisions for sustainable development.
